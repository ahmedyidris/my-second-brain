# Jarvis X Content Engine

Income stream #3 for [[jarvis-x]] (after Outlier + gigs). Fully automated bilingual content pipeline using free-tier tools. Focus niches: Arabic-language AI explainers (hugely under-served) and bilingual tech shorts.

## Pipeline

Topic queue in vault → script drafted by [[the-council]] → voiceover via [[jarvis-x-voice-engine]] → assembly via ffmpeg templates (stock/AI images + subtitles, AR & EN) → human approval → publish.

## Content types

| Type | Platform | Monetization |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Blog posts | Medium, Substack | Affiliate, CTAs |
| Twitter threads | X/Twitter | Affiliate, consulting hooks |
| YouTube scripts | YouTube | Ad revenue |
| Newsletters | Substack, Beehiiv | Paid subscriptions |

## Honest math

YouTube requires 1k subs + 4k watch-hours (or 10M Shorts views) before payouts — expect months of $0, then compounding. Treat all revenue as bonus on top of Outlier + gigs, never instead of them.

## Music note

Free AI-music tiers don't grant commercial rights. Don't monetize music until a paid tier is justified by revenue — treat as content garnish only.

## The moat

Egyptian Arabic is the differentiator. JARVIS X generates its own brand assets as one of its first content-engine jobs — *the system literally brands itself.*
